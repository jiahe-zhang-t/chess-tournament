Competition

Qualification

B Hidden Benchmark
Evaluation against a common set of instructor-side opponents.
R Student League
A round robin against the other valid student agents.

Q = 0.5B + 0.5R

Q determines qualification and playoff seeding. For one valid agent, Q = B; no league, playoffs, or champion.

Random and Greedy are practice opponents and do not contribute to B.

Playoffs

The highest-ranked qualifiers advance. Final placement follows playoff stage, then Q within the same eliminated stage.
