Competition Rules (PDF)Word version

Who your agent plays

Every valid student agent plays 32 games against four instructor-provided opponents, and four games against each other student agent. Each set includes games as White and Black.

Random and Greedy in the Starter are for practice only.

How agents advance

Results against instructor opponents and students each give a percentage score. Their average determines the initial ranking. The top-ranked agents enter the knockout rounds:

Valid student agentsAgents advancing
32 or more16

8 through 154
2 through 72
Fewer than 2No champion

The highest-ranked entrant plays the lowest-ranked entrant, and so on.

With only one valid agent, only instructor-opponent results count. There is no knockout round or champion.

How the champion is decided

Two agents play a series of games. The series winner advances; losing one game does not mean elimination. Each round reduces the field until two agents meet in the final.

The final winner is champion and the other finalist finishes second. Everyone else is ranked by the round they reached, then by their initial score among agents eliminated in that round.

The Rules PDF gives the series lengths, tie procedures, and grading formulas.
